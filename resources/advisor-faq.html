Advisor FAQ

For Faculty Thesis Advisors: CFAN 3091V

What Is This Course?

CFAN 3091V is a 3-credit, A-F honors seminar titled From Ideas to Strategic Plans. Most students develop a Full Thesis Proposal. Students graduating in the current fall semester follow an accelerated thesis-completion track and use the same workshops to revise a complete honors thesis.

The Full Thesis Proposal is different from the UHP Thesis Proposal Form. The form is a shorter administrative submission through the Honors Reporting Center containing the title, brief prospectus, and committee chair. Students are responsible for submitting that form by the applicable UHP deadline; the course does not submit it automatically.

What Do You Need from the Primary Advisor and/or Proposal Committee?

Professional review must be scheduled early. Proposal Development students send the research question, Method and Feasibility Blueprint, and relevant draft sections for an Early Advisor Design Check by Week 10. Fall Graduation students arrange an Early Professional Review of a substantial section or full draft by Week 10. Every student sends the released complete document by Nov 25, before the Week 13 instructor conversation.

The student remains responsible for the writing. Reviewers identify disciplinary problems, unsupported claims, feasibility concerns, missing requirements, and consequential revisions. They are not expected to rewrite the document.

If the Dec 3-9 window is not workable, tell the student when mentorship is confirmed so the student and instructor can establish a viable alternative.

What Is the Honors Thesis?

The University of Minnesota Honors Program requires students to complete a thesis as part of their honors requirements. The thesis:

  • Must be an original scholarly or creative work
  • Must be supervised by a UMN faculty member
  • Must be approved by the student’s honors committee
  • Requirements may vary by department: please check with your unit’s honors coordinator

The Honors Program’s own requirements document is posted here: Honors Requirements, University Honors Program (PDF).

Two committee rules are worth knowing before you agree to serve. The committee has at least three members, a primary advisor and two readers. At least one member must be tenured or tenure-track at the University, though not necessarily the primary advisor, and at least one member must be affiliated with the department in which the thesis is conducted. Non-tenure-track University instructors and researchers with relevant expertise may serve as readers; anyone outside the University requires a departmental petition approved by the Honors Program Director.

How Does This Course Relate to the Thesis Timeline?

Most students take the course one or two semesters before completing the thesis. They follow the Proposal Development Track, and the Dec 16 course document is the Full Thesis Proposal.

Students graduating in Fall 2026 follow the accelerated Fall Graduation Thesis Completion Track. They should already have filed the UHP Thesis Proposal Form, formed the Proposal Committee, and completed enough research to produce a full thesis draft. They file the final thesis and UHP Thesis Completion Form by December 14, 2026. They submit the process portfolio and completion record to the course on December 16, and the committee records review and approval by December 21.

What If My Student’s Research Is Qualitative?

The course covers quantitative, qualitative, and mixed-methods approaches. Module 6 includes a dedicated qualitative track covering interview design, thematic analysis, and qualitative validity concepts. Your student’s proposal will be structured appropriately for their methodology.

What About AI Use?

AI instruction is not part of the CFAN 3091V curriculum, and the course does not use generative AI tools. No assignment requires or expects one. Work students submit must be their own thinking and their own words. The instructor may demonstrate a tool in class at her discretion; that does not authorize student use.

If your own lab or department has a different standard for AI use in thesis work, tell your student. Your requirements govern the thesis; this policy governs what happens in the course.

Template Letter for Students

Students in the course may use the following template when first contacting a potential thesis advisor:

Subject: CFAN 3091V: Honors Thesis Mentorship Inquiry

Dear Professor [Name],

My name is [Student Name], and I am an honors student in [Major] at the University of Minnesota. I am currently enrolled in CFAN 3091V (From Ideas to Strategic Plans), a seminar that guides students through the process of developing a thesis proposal.

I am interested in [brief description of research area/question] and believe your expertise in [specific area of the professor’s work] would be an excellent fit for my project. I have read [specific paper or project] and found [specific aspect] particularly relevant to what I hope to explore.

Would you be willing to meet briefly (20–30 minutes) to discuss whether a mentorship arrangement would work? I am happy to come to your office at a time convenient for you.

Thank you for considering this request. I have attached a brief overview of the course for your reference.

Best regards, [Student Name] [Student Email] [Major and Expected Graduation]
