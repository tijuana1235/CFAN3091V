Week 12: Advisor-ready Document and Release Screen

Whole-Document Review and Professional-Review Send

At a Glance

  • Milestone: 12a
  • Due: before class on Monday, November 23
  • Submission method: Canvas file upload
  • Submit together: advisor-ready document, Whole-Document Revision Memo, and release-screen materials
  • Essential milestone: Yes
  • Then: Milestone 12b, Release and Send Confirmation, due Wednesday, November 25

What This Assignment Is

This milestone is the release gate and the submission point for the instructor’s full-document review. The document does not have to be finished or polished, but it must be readable as one connected argument by someone who is not you.

What Advisor-ready Means

A draft is advisor-ready when:

  • every required section exists in some form, with no placeholder headings;
  • the research question appears in the introduction, stated directly;
  • the literature review is organized around themes and supports the opening your question addresses;
  • the methods would let a reader judge whether the question can be answered;
  • appropriate figures, tables, and captions are present;
  • each retained visual passes the Visual Audit and Caption Clinic stand-alone test;
  • the abstract is drafted; and
  • references are complete and Zotero-generated.

What to Submit

Upload before class on Monday, November 23:

  • the Advisor-ready Full Thesis Proposal or, on the Fall Graduation track, the Advisor-ready Full Thesis Draft;
  • a completed one-page Whole-Document Revision Memo naming the three most important changes made after peer whole-document review, the evidence in the draft that prompted each change, and any issue that still requires expert judgment;
  • the materials required for the release screen.

Sending the released version and confirming it is the separate Milestone 12b, due Wednesday, November 25.

Whole-Document Review

Before the release screen, exchange the complete draft with a peer and trace:

  1. the research question and why it matters;
  2. what the literature establishes, complicates, or leaves unresolved;
  3. how the proposed method answers that exact question;
  4. what outcomes would be distinguishable using that method; and
  5. what limits, feasibility constraints, or program requirements remain.

Instructor Full-Document Review

Submit the complete working draft before class on Monday, November 23. It should include the highest-priority Peer Review 2 revisions you could complete after the November 18 workshop, but it does not need to incorporate every comment. Use the Whole-Document Revision Memo to record what you changed, what you left unresolved, and why.

The instructor’s review focuses on the document’s central argument, question-to-evidence-to-method alignment, expected or actual outcomes, figures and tables, feasibility, and the three highest-priority revisions. The review is discussed during the Week 13 individual conversation; it is not a line edit of every sentence.

Release Sequence

Use the Week 12 workflow in this order:

  1. Upload the advisor-ready package before class on Monday, November 23. That is this milestone, 12a.
  2. Complete the whole-document review and release screen.
  3. Revise the document as needed after that review.
  4. Send the released version to the Primary Advisor and/or Proposal Committee by Wednesday, November 25.
  5. Submit the send confirmation. Steps 4 and 5 are Milestone 12b.

Satisfactory Standard

Every required part must be satisfactory. This milestone is essential for passing the course, and so is Milestone 12b.

Use the full readiness criteria on The Document.

Fall Graduation Track

Apply the same release standard to the complete thesis. Sending the released Advisor-ready Full Thesis Draft for final professional review by November 25 is Milestone 12b.
