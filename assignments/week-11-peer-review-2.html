Week 11: Revised Thesis Draft

Use Peer Feedback to Prepare Your Draft for Instructor Review

At a Glance

  • Due: Wednesday, November 18, at 11:59 p.m. Central Time
  • Submission method: Canvas file upload, PDF or DOCX
  • Submit: your updated and revised thesis proposal or thesis draft only
  • Keep for your own use: the Peer Review 2 worksheet, writer response, and Professional Review Plan; do not upload them for this milestone
  • Instructor feedback: returned no later than Monday, November 23, with the aim of returning it before class

What This Assignment Is

Use the expected-results workshop and peer feedback to revise your draft into one connected document. Monday, November 16 included time for in-class peer review. Meet independently with a new peer reviewer on or before Wednesday, November 18, early enough to use the feedback before submitting.

Wednesday is independent study and writing; there is no regular class meeting. Use that time to incorporate Peer Review 2 feedback and prepare the draft for instructor review. Keep the review worksheet and Professional Review Plan to guide your work. They are not required submissions.

Required Sections

Include these parts in order:

  1. Title
  2. Abstract
  3. Introduction, including the project rationale, research question, and hypothesis
  4. Materials and Methods
  5. Timeline. A table is fine. If the study start date is not yet known, estimate the start and subsequent activities and label the dates as estimates.
  6. Expected Results
  7. Discussion of Expected Results. Explain how the possible outcomes relate to your hypothesis, what they would mean, and how they support the rationale for doing the project. Consider outcomes that do not support the hypothesis.
  8. Limitations or Potential Limitations
  9. Conclusion
  10. Works Cited

You may use labeled placeholders for expected-results tables and figures in this draft. Identify what each planned visual will show. We will work on figures, tables, and captions in the November 23 workshop. Write the expected-results explanation now; do not invent observations or present anticipated outcomes as collected data.

Submission Format

  • Times New Roman, 12-point font
  • Double-spaced text
  • Continuous line numbers throughout the document
  • Page numbers
  • Your first and last name in the header or footer

Check that the line numbers, page numbers, and name remain visible in the file you upload. Submit one PDF or DOCX through Canvas by 11:59 p.m. on November 18.

Use Peer Review 2

Use the Peer Review 2 worksheet to check the question, methods, outcomes, discussion, and limitations. Point to specific pages or lines and explain the most consequential revision. Discuss the feedback with your new reviewer, then decide what to revise and why. Keep the worksheet and your response for your own use.

Use the Professional Review Plan to confirm who will review the next version, how you will send it, and when feedback can be returned. That plan also stays with you.

What Happens Next

I will review drafts on Thursday and Friday, November 19 and 20, and return comments no later than Monday, November 23, ideally before class. The November 23 class and Milestone 12a remain scheduled as published.

Wednesday, November 25 is independent writing. Revise using my comments and the November 23 workshop, then email the updated document to your Primary Advisor and/or Proposal Committee no later than 4:59 p.m. Central Time on November 25. BCC me at sswells@umn.edu so I can record Milestone 12b. Ask your reviewer to return comments as soon as possible, in time for the December 9 class on understanding and responding to advisor feedback.

Satisfactory Standard

The milestone is satisfactory when:

  • the revised draft contains every required section, with labeled placeholders allowed for expected-results tables and figures;
  • the question, hypothesis, methods, expected outcomes, and discussion form a connected argument for doing the project;
  • the timeline gives a workable sequence and labels estimated dates;
  • limitations are explicit and predictions remain distinct from actual data;
  • the draft shows considered use of peer feedback; and
  • the submitted file follows all formatting instructions above.

The worksheet, writer response, and Professional Review Plan are not assessed as separate submissions for this milestone.

Fall Graduation Track

Submit the revised thesis draft with actual Results and Discussion in place of expected results. Use the timeline for remaining thesis work and completion steps. Report the evidence you have; the allowance for expected-results visual placeholders does not replace actual results. The submission format and November 18 deadline are the same for both tracks.
