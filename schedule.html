Course Schedule

From Ideas to Strategic Plans | Fall 2026

Class meets Mondays and Wednesdays from 10:15 to 11:30 a.m. in Borlaug Hall 213. Instruction runs from Tuesday, September 8 through Wednesday, December 16, 2026.

Most class meetings combine 15 to 25 minutes of focused instruction with 35 to 45 minutes of guided or independent application. The remaining time is for discussion, decisions, and the next step. You should expect to write, revise, search, analyze, or confer during class rather than listen for 75 minutes.

Choose the Correct Track

Most students follow the Proposal Development Track. Students graduating in Fall 2026 who have already filed the University Honors Program (UHP) Thesis Proposal Form and are actively completing the thesis follow the accelerated Fall Graduation Thesis Completion Track. Track placement is confirmed with the instructor in Week 1.

Both tracks begin with Milestone 1: the Course-Track Survey and an Interest, Motivation, and Direction Freewrite. Submit the survey in Canvas by Friday, September 11. Complete it before the first meeting on September 9 if you can, since it helps confirm your track; it earns participation credit when submitted. Bring the Freewrite to class on Monday, September 14, and hand it in after the topic-funnel activity. Fall Graduation students complete the reflection through the thesis they are already finishing. If you are unsure which track fits, say so on the survey, come to the first meeting, and we will sort it out there.

Both tracks use the same instruction and workshops. The difference is the document advanced through those workshops:

  • Proposal Development students build a Full Thesis Proposal.
  • Fall Graduation students revise a complete honors thesis and prepare for the UHP completion process.

Fall Graduation Thesis Completion Track

This accelerated track is for students graduating in Fall 2026. It assumes that the UHP Thesis Proposal Form was filed by June 30, a Primary Advisor and Proposal Committee are already in place, and the research project is far enough along to support thesis completion. A missing proposal form, committee, or viable body of thesis work requires an individual plan with the instructor and Primary Advisor.

Class meets on the same days and at the same times as the Proposal Development Track. This table lists one row per week rather than one per session, because the instruction is shared and only the document differs.

Semester Dates and Closures

NoteSchedule Notes
  • Monday generally frames the week’s problem and begins application. Wednesday generally extends the application through writing, analysis, workshop, or review.
  • Week 1 has one 75-minute meeting. It includes orientation, the course and UHP process, early notice of professional-review responsibilities, and a first paper dissection. The reading skill returns in Weeks 4, 6, and 8 rather than being treated as a one-day lesson.
  • There is no class meeting on Wed Oct 28 because the instructor is out of the office. Week 8 Monday supplies the model and matrix launch; students complete the synthesis drafting and self-diagnosis sequence independently on Wednesday and submit through Canvas.
  • Individual meetings in Weeks 3, 7, and 13 replace regular class meetings. Each of those weeks has its own sign-up, linked from that week’s module page; do not assume a slot carries over from an earlier meeting week. At the enrollment cap the meetings need more time than the two class periods provide, so some appointments fall outside class hours, and an accessible structured asynchronous alternative is available. The remaining course time is assigned to the project work named in the schedule.
  • The professional-review appointment must be arranged well before the full draft exists. Proposal Development students complete an Early Advisor Design Check by Week 10. Fall Graduation students obtain an Early Professional Review of a substantial section or full draft by Week 10. The released full document is sent by Nov 25, and reviewers should plan to return feedback around Dec 9.
  • Two student peer reviews teach students to give and use feedback. The Week 14 professional review is conducted by the Primary Advisor and/or Proposal Committee, not by classmates.
  • The UHP Thesis Proposal Form, the Full Thesis Proposal course assignment, and the completed honors thesis are three different documents with different deadlines.
  • There is no final exam. Proposal Development students submit the final proposal and process portfolio on Dec 16. Fall Graduation students submit the thesis and UHP completion materials on Dec 14, then submit the process portfolio and completion-submission record on Dec 16.
  • This schedule is subject to change. The current version will always be on this course site.
