Week 7: One-on-One Meetings

Phase 2: Evidence and Methods

Learning Objectives

By the end of this week, you should be able to:

  • state your refined research question using Population, Intervention, Comparison, and Outcome (PICO) or a disciplinary equivalent;
  • name the paradigm your question requires and say why;
  • describe the themes emerging from your literature and where they conflict;
  • identify the claim or source in your evidence base that needs the most scrutiny; and
  • name what stands between you and a complete proposal or thesis.

Purpose

This week, regular class is replaced by individual meetings. This is your mid-semester check-in. You should have a confirmed Primary Advisor, or a plan to confirm one, a refined research question, a growing literature base, and an initial methods direction.

Monday and Wednesday: Individual Meetings

Format: 15-minute individual meetings. Sign up for a slot on Canvas. As in Week 3, the two class sessions do not hold every meeting at this length, so some slots fall outside the class hour. Both kinds appear in the same sign-up sheet.

What to Bring

Your Progress Report 3 serves as the meeting agenda. See the Progress Reports page for the template and what counts as satisfactory. It should address:

  1. Mentor status: Confirmed? Meeting scheduled? Need help?
  2. Research question: Your current refined version (PICO or equivalent)
  3. Literature: Where are you? How many sources? What themes are emerging?
  4. Methods thinking: What paradigm? What approach are you considering?
  5. Blueprint: Bring the Method and Feasibility Blueprint and name the design decision that most needs advisor review.
  6. Evidence quality: Which claim or source currently needs the most scrutiny?
  7. Early expert review: Who will review the design, what will you send, and by what date?
  8. Concerns: What is your biggest challenge right now?

What We Will Discuss

  • Your progress toward a complete proposal
  • Methods selection: I can help you think through options
  • Which evidence and design questions need to be resolved next
  • The material and timing for the Early Advisor Design Check
  • Timeline check: are you on track?

When You Are Not Meeting with Me

Use this time to:

  • Build the source notes the Week 8 synthesis matrix runs on. One row per source, with the method and principal finding recorded while the paper is still in front of you.
  • Continue the literature review toward the Week 8 Literature Synthesis Draft.
  • Meet with or email your Primary Advisor.
  • Draft the methods thinking you will need for the Week 10 Introduction, Literature Review, and Methods Draft. It is not due in Week 8, but the reasoning is easier to write down now, while the methods conversation from your meeting is fresh.

Milestone Due

  • Progress Report 3: bring one printed copy to your meeting and hand it in afterward. The result will appear in the Canvas gradebook; do not upload it.

Fall Graduation Thesis Completion Track

Bring printed copies of Thesis Progress Report 2 and a Professional Review Plan, then hand them in after the meeting. The plan names the Primary Advisor and/or Proposal Committee, the material for an Early Professional Review by Week 10, the Nov 25 full-draft delivery deadline, the expected feedback date around Dec 9, and the UHP completion and committee-approval deadlines. We will also evaluate analysis status, missing evidence, feasibility, and the path to a complete Week 12 thesis draft.
